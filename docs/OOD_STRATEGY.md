# Out-of-Distribution Strategy

OOD does not mean an unconstrained promise to solve every GMAT mission. It means the search and guards are deliberately varied beyond the Workshop geometry.

## Covered distribution shifts

- arbitrary inclination and RAAN differences;
- eccentric initial orbits;
- prograde and retrograde transfer planes;
- short/long transfer directions;
- zero through configured multiple revolutions;
- exact antipodal Lambert singularity;
- zero-burn natural interception;
- one-burn infeasibility under 1.5 km/s;
- two-burn global fallback;
- hidden between-sample 5 km crossing;
- impulsive duplicate-epoch velocity discontinuity;
- unexpected ForceModel richness (warning and mandatory GMAT gate).

## Defense in depth

1. Deterministic grid landmarks include the organizer timeline.
2. Scrambled Sobol points cover continuous timing/control variables.
3. Universal-variable solutions are independently propagated.
4. A Cartesian shooting fallback covers collinear endpoint geometry.
5. The candidate archive retains time/delta-v tradeoffs instead of one guessed optimum.
6. GMAT verification is independent and process isolated.
7. Unknown Script syntax or permissions fail closed.

## Not covered without extension

- finite burns;
- non-Earth central bodies in the current parser policy;
- mismatched spacecraft epochs;
- formation objects or more than two spacecraft;
- dynamically maneuvering target;
- command graphs other than the supported alternating template.
